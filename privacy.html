Privacy notice

Last updated November 9, 2026

Who we are

PATHOS is a research project led by Rahul Viswanath in Bentonville, Arkansas. Questions about this notice go to rsignsv1279@gmail.com.

What the contact form collects

  • Your name and email address
  • Your organization, if you give one
  • The type of interest you choose, and your message

We use these details to reply to you about your message.

How your message reaches us

The form is delivered by FormSubmit, which forwards it by email to the PATHOS inbox. FormSubmit handles your message under its own terms and privacy policy while delivering it.

What this site does not collect

This website collects no physiological data, and the PATHOS prototype is not connected to it. The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising trackers.

To serve the pages, our host Vercel processes standard request information such as your IP address and browser type. Fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser also contacts Google.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete a message you sent, or ask what we hold about you, by emailing rsignsv1279@gmail.com.

Not a medical service

PATHOS is a wellness awareness research prototype. It is not a medical device, it does not diagnose, and nothing on this site is medical advice.
